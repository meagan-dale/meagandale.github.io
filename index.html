Meet Penny 🐾

Penny is the face of Pawsitive Excursions and a reminder of what this business is all about: getting dogs out into the world, giving them meaningful experiences, and helping them become confident, happy companions.

Every outing is tailored to the individual dog. Some dogs need a peaceful sniffing adventure. Some need structured leash work. Some need distance from other dogs. The goal is always a safe, productive and enjoyable experience.
